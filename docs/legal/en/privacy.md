# Privacy Policy — Jobetes

Last updated: 2026-05-04

This Privacy Policy explains how **Dr. Mahmoud Al-Shdaifat** (the "Controller"), in connection with the *Jobetes* portal, processes your personal data. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), §203 of the German Criminal Code, and Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) 2023.

## 1. Who is responsible

Dr. Mahmoud Al-Shdaifat — Klinik für Gastroenterologie, St. Anna Hospital Herne, Hospitalstraße 19, 44649 Herne, Germany. Contact: `wanderwellcare@gmail.com`.

## 2. What we collect

When you submit a patient intake we collect:

- **Identity:** name, date of birth, gender, preferred language, phone, optional email.
- **Health data (special category, Art. 9 GDPR):** symptoms, severity, duration, medications, allergies, conditions.
- **Cultural context:** doctor-gender preference, fasting status, Ramadan context.
- **Consent records:** which version of this policy you accepted, in which language, at what time.

When you contact the clinic, we additionally process correspondence content.

## 3. Why we process it

- **Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — contract performance:** preparing and delivering the consultation you requested.
- **Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR — explicit consent for health data:** you give this in the intake form, separately from the general Terms.
- **Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR — legal obligation:** medical record-keeping where applicable.

## 4. Who else sees your data

- **Sub-processors** (DPA in place): Netlify (web hosting), Fly.io (API hosting in Frankfurt), Google (AI triage). Their role is bounded by contract; they do not use your data for their own purposes.
- **The clinic staff** who need access to plan your consultation.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Cross-border transfer

Your data may be processed in Germany and in EU data centers. Transfer between Jordan and Germany is covered by your explicit consent and applicable safeguards.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Phase 0 (current):** intake records are processed in-memory only and not persisted across server restarts.
- **Phase 1 (with database):** retained 24 months from your last contact, unless legal obligations require longer.

## 7. Your rights

You can at any time:

- request **access** to your data (Art. 15)
- request **correction** (Art. 16)
- request **erasure** (Art. 17 — "right to be forgotten")
- request **data portability** (Art. 20)
- **object** to processing (Art. 21)
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing
- lodge a **complaint** with a supervisory authority (Germany: LDI NRW; Jordan: Personal Data Protection Council)

To exercise rights, write to `wanderwellcare@gmail.com`.

## 8. Security

TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest (Phase 1), strict access control, audit logs with PII redaction. Details in our `SECURITY.md`.

## 9. Changes

We will publish substantive changes here and request renewed consent where required.

## 10. AI features — limited risk

The triage tool is an EU AI Act *limited-risk* feature. It does **not** diagnose. It suggests urgency categories and topics for your consultation. In emergencies, dial **112** (Germany) or **911** (Jordan).
